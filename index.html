N°04 — ORIGIN · LOS ALTOS, JALISCO

FROM EARTH TO SPIRIT.

Seven years in iron-rich soil. One estate. No shortcuts. Scroll through the land that makes the bottle possible.

The Highlands give Aguavida its high, floral lift — white pepper, lime leaf, wet stone.

We harvest only at full maturity, when the piña holds its sugars like a held breath. Everything after that is restraint.
